## Profile

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at Politecnico di Torino (PLANET lab), working on AI-driven optical networks. My current focus is an AI-enabled digital twin for optical network design and planning, with LLMs integrated into the design and monitoring tools, together with AI for network monitoring and restoration. I'm also working on a fibre sensing project that uses State-of-Polarisation monitoring to detect and localise mechanical threats to fibre cables, with LLM-based support for network operators. More broadly, my research covers machine learning for failure prediction and anomaly detection, integrated sensing and communication in live metro networks, converged metro-access networks for 6G x-haul, and capacity scaling for future transport networks. My background is in multi-band transmission and ROADM/WSS architectures, and I work mainly with Python, MATLAB, C/C++, TensorFlow and GNPy. I received the IEEE Photonics Society Italian Chapter Best PhD Thesis Award (2024) and the Ambassador of Pakistan High Achievers Award (2026).

## Research interests

Optical communication networks, multi-band optical transport, photonic integrated switching, WSS and ROADM architectures, machine learning for optical networks, optical performance monitoring, fibre sensing and fault detection, QoT estimation, AI-driven network management, generative AI for telecom operations
